About the Image Resizer

JScale is a free image resizer that works on many images at once. It crops and resizes every image to the exact size you pick, or keeps each image's own size and only converts the format. Everything happens in your browser: your images are never uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Pick an output size: 1200 × 628 (blog and landing page images), 640 × 420, Original to keep each image's size, or type your own width and height.
  2. Drop your images on the page, or click to browse.
  3. Check the crop. Click any image in the strip to edit it, then drag the crop box (or use the arrow keys) to choose which part is kept.
  4. Choose quality and format, then Download All (Ctrl + Shift + D). You can also save single images and rename files first.

What it supports

  • Input: JPG, PNG, WebP and HEIC (iPhone photos).
  • Output: JPG or WebP, at low, medium or high quality. Safari can't save WebP, so there it saves JPG.
  • Sizes: presets, any custom size from 32 to 20,000 pixels (your last five are kept as shortcuts), or original size.
  • Saving: one download per image, a single ZIP file, or straight into a folder you choose (Chrome and Edge).

Private by design

Resizing, cropping and converting all run on your own device, so it works with client and unreleased images, and it's fast even with large batches. Nothing is sent to a server.
