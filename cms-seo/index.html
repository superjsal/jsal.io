About CMS SEO Checker

CMS SEO Checker answers the question that comes up on almost every SEO project: can we actually change that on this platform? It lists what WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Framer, HubSpot CMS, BigCommerce, Magento, Drupal, Duda, GoDaddy and headless CMSs (Sanity, Contentful, Strapi and similar) let you change, so you can compare them side by side before you plan a fix.

How to read it

  • Each row is one thing you might need to change: page titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, 301 redirects, canonical tags, structured data, the XML sitemap and theme or template code.
  • The tags run from Built in and Automatic through Needs plugin/app, Needs code and Needs hosting/DNS to Not possible. Check manually means the answer depends on something that can't be told from outside.
  • Select a platform name for its profile: what kind of platform it is, who it suits, who it doesn't, and what it's known for.

Good to know

These answers are general guidance. Plans, themes, apps and permissions change what is possible on a given site, and platforms update often. It says what is possible, not how to do it.
