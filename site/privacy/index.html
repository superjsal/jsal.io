Your privacy

The tools here run in your browser: images, documents and anything you paste never leave your device. The only thing sent anywhere is basic usage stats through Google Analytics, and in Europe only if you allow it.

Google Analytics

It tells me which pages and tools get used. It records:

  • pages viewed, how you arrived (a search, a link) and roughly how long you stayed
  • your device, browser and approximate location (city level, worked out from your IP address; Google Analytics 4 doesn’t log or store the address itself)
  • a few actions: finishing the CMS Picker quiz (and its top match), opening a platform page, adding a platform to the comparison, and clicking a platform’s help link

No ads, no ad profiles: Google signals and advertising links are off, and no names, emails or user IDs are sent.

Cookies. _ga and _ga_ND4L6CSD8S tell one visit from another. They last up to 2 years.

How long data is kept. Detailed event data for 2 months; data tied to a visitor for up to 14 months, reset when you come back. Totals in reports (like page views per month) are kept longer.

In Europe (EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, going by your browser’s time zone), Google’s script doesn’t load at all until you choose Allow. Everywhere else it loads. If Google places you in Europe anyway (say, on a VPN), it sets no cookies and only sends cookieless signals until you allow it. You can turn it off below.

More on how Google handles this data: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Changing your choice

Your choice is kept in this browser.

Choosing “No thanks” stops Google Analytics and removes its cookies. The same button is in the footer of the home page.

Kept in your browser only

A few settings are saved in your browser’s storage and never sent anywhere: light or dark theme, achievements and which tools you’ve opened, your analytics choice, CMS Picker’s Show notes, and the Image Resizer’s settings and the save folder you picked. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (including IP addresses) for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Platform Playbook

The team-only Platform Playbook is separate: you sign in with Discord, and only people I approve can see it. When you ask for access it keeps your Discord ID, name and avatar so I can approve you; members’ details and the team’s edits are kept in a Supabase database, for the team’s use only. Ask me to remove yours at any time. It has no analytics.
