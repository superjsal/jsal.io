About the Image Resizer

Exact sizes for one image or a batch, with a crop you control, and nothing ever leaves your device.

How to use it

  1. Pick an output size: 1200 × 628 (blog posts and social share images), 640 × 420 (smaller feature images), Original to keep each image's size, or type your own width and height.
  2. Drop your images on the page, or click to browse.
  3. Check the crop. Click any image in the strip to edit it, then drag the crop box (or use the arrow keys) to choose which part is kept.
  4. Choose quality and format, then Download All (Ctrl + Shift + D). You can also save single images and rename files first.

What it supports
  • Input: JPG, PNG, WebP and HEIC (iPhone photos).
  • Output: JPG or WebP at low, medium or high quality, or PNG (lossless, keeps transparency, biggest files). Safari can't save WebP, so there it saves JPG.
  • Sizes: presets, any custom size from 32 to 20,000 pixels (your last five are kept as shortcuts), or original size.
  • Saving: one download per image, a single ZIP file, or straight into a folder you choose (Chrome and Edge).

Common questions

Is it free?
Yes. There's no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many images you resize.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes, in any up-to-date browser on iPhone or Android. Pick images from your photos or files; they save as downloads or a ZIP.
