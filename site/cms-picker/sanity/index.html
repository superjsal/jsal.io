Is Sanity right for you?

The short version, from the CMS Picker profile.

Type
Headless CMS
Starts at
Free plan; the website’s hosting is separate
Hosting and updates
Sanity hosts the content; a developer hosts the website
Take with you
Everything: your content and files in one archive with the Sanity CLI; the website’s code is already yours

A good fit for

  • Companies with developers building custom sites or apps
  • Content reused across a website, app and other channels
  • Teams who edit together in real time

Less suited when

  • There's no developer
  • Marketers need to change layout or SEO settings without developer help (unless the site was built for it)

What’s built in

  • Run a blogNeeds codevariesPost types the developer builds (blog starter templates exist)
  • Sell memberships or paid postsNeeds codevariesBuilt by the developer with a membership service
  • Sell onlineNeeds codevariesPaired with a store: Sanity Connect syncs Shopify products
  • Use more than one languagePlugin/appvariesSanity’s own internationalization plugins
  • Add contact and sign-up formsNeeds codevariesBuilt by the developer or a form service
  • Design every detailNeeds codeYes: the whole front end is custom-built
