About CMS Picker

CMS Picker helps you choose a website builder or CMS (content management system) by answering two questions that come up on almost every project: which platform fits this business? and can we actually change that on it?

How to read it
  • Find your fit asks a few quick questions and ranks the platforms, with the reasons from the answers below. Nothing you choose is stored or sent anywhere; the result is in the page address, so you can share it.
  • Each card says what kind of platform it is, who it’s for, what it’s known for, who looks after hosting and updates, what you can take with you if you leave, and when it’s not the right choice.
  • Each row of the comparison is a plain question, with its SEO name in small text under it.
  • The tags run from Built in and Automatic through Plugin/app and Needs code to Not possible. Unclear means the platform’s docs don’t say.

Good to know

Answers come from each platform’s own documentation and are rechecked every few months. Anything not checked yet is marked not verified.

The profiles and answers are general guidance. Plans, themes, apps and permissions change what is possible on a given site, and platforms update often. For SEO, each answer also says where to find the setting.
