Is HubSpot CMS right for you?

The short version, from the CMS Picker profile.

A good fit for

  • Businesses already using HubSpot’s CRM or marketing tools
  • B2B lead generation
  • Marketing teams that want forms and CRM data built in

Less suited when

  • You don’t otherwise use HubSpot (cost)
  • You need an online store
  • You want paid memberships or a paid newsletter (members-only pages need Content Hub Professional, and payments aren’t tied to access)

What’s built in

  • Run a blogBuilt inHubSpot blog, on every plan (free tools included)
  • Sell memberships or paid postsUnclearvariesMembers-only pages need Content Hub Professional; tying payment to access takes a workflow
  • Sell onlinePlugin/appNo storefront: payment links and invoices from a product library; a full store needs an integration (e.g. Shopify)
  • Use more than one languageBuilt invariesLanguage versions of pages and posts (free tools: up to 3)
  • Add contact and sign-up formsBuilt inHubSpot forms, saved straight to the CRM
  • Design every detailNeeds codeYes, with a developer building themes and modules
