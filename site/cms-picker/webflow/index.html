Is Webflow right for you?

The short version, from the CMS Picker profile.

Type

The site’s code on paid Workspace plans; content collections (CMS) as spreadsheets

A good fit for

  • Design-led marketing sites
  • Teams with a designer but no developer to write code
  • Marketing teams publishing through a CMS

Less suited when

  • You run a large online store
  • You want simple drag-and-drop without design skills

What’s built in

  • Run a blogBuilt invariesBuilt with its content manager (CMS Collections); how many posts depends on the site plan
  • Sell memberships or paid postsPlugin/appIts own memberships were retired in 2026; apps like Memberstack or Outseta
  • Sell onlineBuilt invariesOn Ecommerce site plans: 500 to 15,000 products, variants and categories
  • Use more than one languageBuilt invariesWebflow Localize, its own paid add-on (from $9/mo per language; not with Ecommerce plans)
  • Add contact and sign-up formsBuilt invariesUnlimited submissions on paid plans (50 in total on the free plan)
  • Design every detailBuilt inYes, visually, without code
