Is Square Online right for you?

The short version, from the CMS Picker profile.

Type
Website and online store builder
Starts at
Free plan with a .square.site address; your own domain needs the Plus plan ($49/mo)
Hosting and updates
Handled for you
Take with you
Products as a spreadsheet, from Square’s Item library; the site itself can’t be taken with you

A good fit for

  • Shops, cafés and restaurants already using Square
  • Selling in your shop and online with the same stock
  • Small shops starting free

Less suited when

  • You need paid memberships
  • You want full design control or control over how pages show in search
  • You have a large product catalogue

What’s built in

  • Run a blogBuilt inBuilt in, called Stories
  • Sell memberships or paid postsNot possibleNo member accounts or paid access; only password-protected pages, on the Plus plan or higher
  • Sell onlineBuilt inBuilt in, sharing products and stock with Square’s in-store checkout
  • Use more than one languagePlugin/appvariesWith the Weglot Translate app, or a separate site per language, on Plus or higher
  • Add contact and sign-up formsBuilt inContact form sections, with entries in the dashboard
  • Design every detailNot possibleA theme and sections you arrange; your own code only in embed sections, on the Plus plan or higher
