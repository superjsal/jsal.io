About CMS Picker

CMS Picker helps you choose a website builder or CMS (content management system), and see what each means for getting found on Google and in AI search. It covers WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Framer, HubSpot CMS, BigCommerce, Magento, Drupal, Duda, Ghost and headless CMSs (Sanity, Contentful, Strapi and similar). It answers two questions that come up on almost every project: which platform fits this business? and can we actually change that on it?

How to read it

  • Not sure where to start? Find your fit asks a few quick questions and ranks the platforms for you, with the reasons from the answers below. Nothing you choose is stored or sent anywhere; the result is in the page address, so you can share it.
  • Or pick what you’re building to narrow the list. Each card says what kind of platform it is, who it’s for, what it’s known for, and when it’s not the right choice. The Headless CMS card covers Sanity, Contentful, Strapi and similar.
  • Each card also says who looks after hosting and updates, and what you can take with you if you leave.
  • The comparison has two parts: Everyday (a blog, an online store, more than one language, forms, design control) and Getting found on Google and AI search (titles and descriptions, page addresses, redirects, structured data, keeping a page out of search, and AI crawlers). Each row is a plain question, with its SEO name in small text under it.
  • The tags run from Built in and Automatic through Plugin/app and Needs code to Not possible. Unclear means the platform’s docs don’t say.

Good to know

The profiles and answers are general guidance. Plans, themes, apps and permissions change what is possible on a given site, and platforms update often. It says what is possible, not how to do it.
